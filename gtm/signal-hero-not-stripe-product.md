## Key Messaging Pillars

1. **WHO**: SaaS founders who bill on Stripe (not "Stripe users" — too broad)
2. **WHAT**: Read cancel/silent-churn signals → draft save → enroll Resend segment after approval
3. **NOT**: Made by Stripe / Stripe Dashboard / Connect app / cancel widget / auto-send tool
4. **PROOF POINTS**:
   - Human in the loop (no auto-sends)
   - Resend segments with ret_* codes (technical specificity)
   - No cancel-button widget (not invasive)
   - No email from our domain (white-label, your brand)

---

## Anti-Patterns (Do Not Use)

❌ "Connect your Stripe account" (OAuth vibes)
❌ "Stripe churn solution" (implies Stripe made it)
❌ "Works with Stripe" (too generic, could be Dashboard)
❌ "Stripe integration" (too vague)
❌ "Powered by Stripe" (implies partnership)
❌ "For Stripe customers" (ambiguous — us or their customers?)

✅ "For SaaS founders who bill on Stripe" (clear relationship)
✅ "Independent product — not made by Stripe" (explicit)
✅ "Your Resend. Human approval." (ownership clarity)
